Our story

We Are Holidae

More than a travel agency we are architects of extraordinary moments, dedicated to crafting journeys that transform and inspire.

How it began

Born From a Passion for Discovery

Holidae was founded in 2012 by two lifelong explorers who believed the travel industry was failing the people it was meant to serve. Too many generic packages, too little genuine care, and far too few truly unforgettable experiences.

Starting with a small office in Dubai, our founders set out to build something different a travel company where every single client felt like their journey was the only one that mattered. Because to us, it is.

Today, Holidae is a team of over 45 passionate travel specialists operating across 6 cities, with access to 200+ destinations worldwide. But our ethos has never changed: every trip we design is personal, purposeful, and extraordinary.
